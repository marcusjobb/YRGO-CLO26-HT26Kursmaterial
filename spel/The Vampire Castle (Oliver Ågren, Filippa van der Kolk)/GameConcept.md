**Game idea: The Vampire Castle**

* **Theme:** The Vampire Castle  
* **Goal:** Save your friend being held hostage by a vampire in the room the furthest away in the castle  
* **Overview:** The player finds themselves in an old vampire castle and must make their way through several rooms. In each room, the player encounters a vampire who blocks the way forward. To get the key to the next room, the player must solve a simple math question that the vampire asks.  
* **Gameplay \- The castle consists of several rooms arranged in a sequence.**  
  **Each room contains:**  
  * A vampire  
  * A math question  
  * A key that is unlocked when the player answers correctly  
  * Incorrect answers result in a lost game \- the vampire in the room will kill you\!  
* **The Final Room**  
  * To save his friend in the last room, the player must solve a final math question to get a key to the rescue room and an antidote for the friend.  
* **End result**  
  **The game combines:**  
  * A clear adventure theme  
  * A progression through rooms  
  * Mathematics as a game mechanic  
  * A final goal that ties the story together
